Game Rules

Fuzhounese Mahjong (福州麻将)

Introduction

Fuzhounese Mahjong is a popular variation of traditional Mahjong played with 144 tiles. It is a fast-paced game that heavily features Flower replacement (补花) and a dynamic Wildcard (Jin / 金).

The Tile Set

  • Suit Tiles: Characters (万), Dots (饼), and Bamboos (条) numbered 1-9 (108 tiles).
  • Flower Tiles (36 tiles):
    • Winds: East (东), South (南), West (西), North (北) (16 tiles)
    • Dragons: Red (中), Green (发), White (白) (12 tiles)
    • Seasons: Spring (春), Summer (夏), Autumn (秋), Winter (冬) (4 tiles)
    • Plants: Plum (梅), Orchid (兰), Bamboo (竹), Chrysanthemum (菊) (4 tiles)

Note: In Fuzhounese Mahjong, Winds and Dragons are treated as Flower tiles. They cannot be held in the hand or form normal melds; they must be declared and replaced (补花) immediately upon drawing.

The Wildcard (Jin / 金)

The defining mechanic of Fuzhou Mahjong is the Jin (金), a wildcard tile that can substitute for any suit tile to form a meld (Chow, Pung, or Kong).

Determining the Jin

After dealing and initial flower replacement, the dealer flips over a tile from the back of the wall (the Jin Indicator).

  • The tile identical to the indicator becomes the Jin. For example, if a 4 of Dots is flipped, then all remaining 4 of Dots in play are Jin tiles.
  • If a Flower tile is flipped as the indicator, it is automatically given to the dealer, and another tile is flipped from the back until a suit tile is revealed.

Special Rules for Jin

  • A Jin tile in the hand glows gold to signify its wildcard status.
  • You cannot discard a Jin tile unless you hold 4 Jin tiles or are declaring a special win condition.

Winning Hand Conditions

To declare a win (Hu / 胡), a player's hand must consist of 4 melds and 1 pair (17 tiles in hand/melds total). Melds are Chow (runs), Pung (triplets), or Kong (quads). The Jin can substitute for any suit tile.

Special Winning Hands

  • San Jin Dao (三金倒): Having exactly 3 Jin tiles in hand is an automatic instant win, regardless of the rest of the hand structure.
  • Jin Que (金雀 - Gold Sparrow): A winning hand where two Jin tiles are used specifically as the eye/pair (雀头).
  • Jin Long (金龙 - Gold Dragon): A winning hand where three Jin tiles are used to form a triplet (Pung).
  • Qiang Jin (抢金 - Robbing Jin): Drawing a Jin tile on the player's very first turn of the game results in an automatic instant win.
  • No Jin (无金): Winning the hand without using any Jin tiles as wildcards (increases your multiplier).

Action Priority

When a tile is discarded, actions are prioritized in this order: Hu (胡) > Pung (碰) / Kong (杠) > Chow (吃). Chow can only be declared from the player directly to your left.

Score (水 / Points) Calculation

Fuzhou scoring is calculated by summing base elements (points or "water") and applying a multiplier based on the win condition:

Total Score = (Base + Flowers + Jins + Kongs + Dealer Streak) × Win Multiplier

Base Points

  • Base: 3 points (底分).
  • Flower Tiles: +1 point per flower. A complete set of 4 identical winds/seasons/plants counts as 6 flowers.
  • Jins in Hand: +1 point per Jin tile.
  • Kongs: Ming Kong (+1), An Kong (+2), Wind/Dragon Kong (+2).
  • Dealer Streak (连庄): Dynamic points depending on how many consecutive rounds the dealer has won/held.

Multipliers (番数)

  • Ping Hu (平胡): 1x (discarded by opponent)
  • Self-Drawn (自摸): 2x (drawn from wall by winner)
  • No Jin (无金): 2x
  • San Jin Dao (三金倒): 3x
  • Jin Que (金雀) / Jin Long (金龙): 4x
  • Robbing Jin (抢金): 5x
